## T-02A round 13 diagnosis (2026-10-24), before the last fit

Step 1, alignment: no problem found. Reference speed samples already get their
`s` by projecting the lap's x/y onto the sim centre line (path-continuous
nearest point; identical to plain nearest point except a few samples at the
crossover). A time offset between OpenF1 `car_data` and `location` was scanned
over ±1.5 s for every team: best offset -0.05 s, RMSE change ≤ 0.4 km/h. A
constant shift of the real trace changes nothing (best -5 m). The five pooled
T-029 laps start -17 to +24 m from the line (within one ~3.7 Hz sample); pooling
places every sample by projection, so this does not move the geometry.

Step 2, coverage: `calibration.min_section_coverage` 22 of 24 sections of
`coverage_section_m` 250 m; the target lap time stays each team's fastest lap,
the trace comes from its fastest covered lap (`load_team_references`). Nine teams
are covered by their fastest lap (22-24 sections). Mercedes and Audi need other
laps that are not cached: OpenF1 refused them (HTTP 401, "Live F1 session in
progress", unauthenticated access is closed during live sessions), so they wait
for the API to reopen.

Step 3, curvature: radius implied by the real median corner minimum (provisional
grip and downforce) against the model radius. The `splprep` centre line had
T8 1.62, T9 0.66, T17 0.61, T2/T4 0.84-0.88 (model / implied); its adaptive knots
cut short sharp corners and sharpen others, non-monotonically in the smoothing
level. A penalized spline (`track_builder.centre_line_fit: penalized`,
`smoothing_lambda_m3` 5000, chosen as the smallest RMS log error over the
grip-limited corners: 0.15, worst 0.29; raw length -0.75 %, closure and length
checks pass) brings the corners close. It stays switched off (`splprep`) until
the refit, because the provisional coefficients belong to the `splprep`
geometry (on the new one laps are 1-2 s faster).

What the RMSE is made of (Ferrari, full trace, new geometry, provisional
coefficients): median RMSE 25.2 km/h against 24.7 on the old geometry, so
geometry is not the main term. 63 % of the squared error comes from errors over
40 km/h, in two places:

1. Esses / Degner 1 / T3 (40-75 km/h too fast): with constant tyre friction the
   corner speed `v² = μ m g / (m κ − μ ½ ρ ClA)` diverges at the radius where
   `m κ = μ ½ ρ ClA`; with the fitted μ 2.15 and ClA 4.84 that is ~124 m. Every
   corner above it is flat, and corners at 85-120 m (the Esses) are near the
   singularity: 287 km/h at R 88 m (7.4 g) where real cars do 233 km/h (4.9 g).
   Real tyres lose friction with load (load sensitivity), which the model lacks.
2. Energy: the T-028 deploy-anywhere policy empties the battery by ~1 870 m
   (Esses), so the second half runs 20-30 km/h slow on the straights. Same lap
   with the T-026 zone policy: RMSE 23.5 instead of 27.2.

Expectation: the last allowed fit on the current model would FAIL again; it has
not been started (owner decision requested).
